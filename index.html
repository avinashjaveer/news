Indian Stock Market Takes a Severe Beating, Wiping Out Retail Investor Portfolios

Market volatility hits hard as benchmark indices crash, leaving investor portfolios battered and retail traders facing heavy losses across sectors.

MUMBAI: Bloodbath continues on Dalal Street as the Indian stock market suffers relentless selling pressure, smashing retail investors' portfolios across small-cap, mid-cap, and blue-chip holdings alike.

Key indices experienced sharp intraday plunges triggered by global macroeconomic headwinds, rising geopolitical tensions, and sustained FII capital outflows. Investors across the country have watched months of steady gains vanish in a matter of trading sessions.

"It has been an absolute carnage for retail portfolios," remarked a senior market strategist. "Every bounce attempt is met with aggressive selling, leaving short-term traders and long-term investors struggling to manage downside risks."
